# Artist–operator data processing agreement review

**Draft checklist only. This file is not an executed agreement and does not
establish acceptance, identities, safeguards or legal compliance.** Complete the
fields, write the actual terms and annexes, and record execution before claiming
an Article 28 agreement exists. Keep this review with [privacy operations](privacy-operations.md).

The independent artist is controller for its client records; the Tinta operator
is processor when hosting those records under instructions. Operator account,
security and own support purposes belong in its controller notice and are not
made processor activities merely by this document.
[EDPB role guidance, paragraphs 71 and 78–81](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/system/files/documents/2023-10/EDPB_guidelines_202007_controllerprocessor_final_en.pdf).

## Required contractual provisions

Include concrete obligations, not only a statement that the parties comply with
GDPR. The Article 28 agreement may be electronic, but must bind identified parties.
[AEPD agreement requirements](https://www.aepd.es/preguntas-frecuentes/2-tus-obligaciones-como-responsable-del-tratamiento/8-responsable-y-encargado-del-tratamiento),
[AEPD drafting guidance](https://www.aepd.es/sites/default/files/2019-10/guia-directrices-contratos.pdf).

- [ ] Process only documented lawful instructions, including international
  transfers; explain legally compelled processing and required prior notice.
- [ ] Bind authorised personnel to confidentiality and limit access to duties.
- [ ] Attach reviewed Article 32 security measures and their responsibilities:
  access control, tenant isolation, private storage, encryption/key assessment,
  patching, availability, recovery, testing and deletion. Describe actual controls
  without promising unimplemented certifications or service levels.
- [ ] Require prior specific or general written authorisation for subprocessors.
  With general authorisation, state advance change notification and a meaningful
  objection process. Pass equivalent obligations to subprocessors; retain the
  processor's responsibility for their performance.
- [ ] Assist the artist with access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability,
  objections and consent withdrawal where applicable; state request routing,
  identity/authority checking and secure response procedures.
- [ ] Assist with security, breach notification, DPIA and prior consultation,
  taking account of the information available to the processor. Notify the artist
  of a breach without undue delay; specify contact/escalation and staged updates.
  Do not give the processor a blanket 72-hour delay before notifying the artist.
- [ ] On ending services, return or delete personal data at the artist's choice
  and delete copies unless applicable law requires retention. Define export
  format, artwork delivery, active-storage deletion, backup expiry/restoration
  suppression and any restricted legal retention; do not invent a universal term.
- [ ] Make compliance information available and support audits/inspections,
  including by an auditor mandated by the artist; define workable coordination.
- [ ] Immediately inform the artist if an instruction infringes applicable data
  protection law and define how unlawful processing is paused/escalated.

## Subprocessor and transfer annex to verify

| Entity / actual role | Data and service | Required evidence / status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cloudflare; classify actual proxy processing | Proxied traffic, connection metadata and potentially client content | Legal entity, terms/DPA, processing/access countries, subprocessor list, artist authorisation and applicable transfer mechanism: **REVIEW** |
| Any separate hosting/storage/backup provider | Exact service/data: **REVIEW** | Operator-owned server is not itself a separate legal subprocessor; identify any actual external provider and locations |
| Google identity | Artist sign-in identity; not Google Drive client storage | Review actual controller/processor role and agreement/transfer terms; do not automatically classify as client-record subprocessor |
| Resend, only if enabled | Artist recovery email and recovery message | Prospective while unconfigured; review entity, contract, countries and safeguards before enabling |
| WhatsApp artist-triggered external link | Message/phone data selected by artist | Separate recipient/disclosure review; a link alone does not establish a Tinta subprocessor relationship |

Add other recipients only when actually used. Outside-EEA access/transfers need a
verified applicable adequacy decision or safeguards. Identify the exact entity,
covered service, mechanism and supporting assessment; generic references to SCCs,
DPF or a vendor brand are not evidence.
[AEPD transfer rules](https://www.aepd.es/derechos-y-deberes/cumple-tus-deberes/medidas-de-cumplimiento/garantias-transferencias-datos-personales).

## Artist responsibilities and execution record

Record the artist's client information, lawful bases, any Article 9 exception,
permitted content and retention instructions. The operator agreement is not
client consent. Review financial-record obligations and, where Spanish law
applies, restricted blocking rather than ordinary reusable soft deletion.
[AEPD client information](https://www.aepd.es/derechos-y-deberes/conoce-tus-derechos/derecho-de-informacion),
[AEPD health-data rules](https://www.aepd.es/areas-de-actuacion/salud/tus-derechos-en-relacion-con-tus-datos-de-salud),
[LOPDGDD Article 32](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2018-16673#a3-4).
